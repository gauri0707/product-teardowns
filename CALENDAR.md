# 13-Week Calendar

**Sep 14 – Dec 11, 2026.** One product per week, Monday through Friday. Weekends are free — use them to build a prototype, or don't.

Domains rotate deliberately so the portfolio doesn't read as one-note: AI/LLM products, fintech and payments, B2B SaaS and dev tools.

---

## Week 1 — Cursor
**Sep 14–18 · AI / dev tools**

The opener, because it's the clearest live experiment in pricing an AI product. Cursor has repriced more than once under real margin pressure, in public, with an audience that reacts loudly. Good material for the pricing thread that runs through the whole quarter.

**Angle:** How do you price a product whose marginal cost is a moving target?

---

## Week 2 — Razorpay
**Sep 21–25 · Fintech (India)**

Merchant onboarding is the whole game in Indian payments, and it's a genuinely hard product problem — KYC, risk, and time-to-first-transaction pulling against each other.

**Angle:** Where does a payments company actually win — the checkout, or the onboarding?

---

## Week 3 — Linear
**Sep 28 – Oct 2 · B2B SaaS**

The canonical opinionated tool. Worth studying because its moat is taste, which is the hardest thing to defend and the easiest to claim.

**Angle:** Is speed a feature or a marketing story? Find the evidence either way.

---

## Week 4 — Perplexity
**Oct 5–9 · AI**

Answer engines have an unsolved economics problem and an unsolved publisher problem, both at once.

**Angle:** What does a sustainable business model for an answer engine look like?

---

## Week 5 — Stripe
**Oct 12–16 · Fintech**

Documentation as a go-to-market channel. The most-copied and least-understood part of the Stripe playbook.

**Angle:** How much of Stripe's win is developer experience, and how much is distribution and timing?

---

## Week 6 — Notion AI
**Oct 19–23 · AI in an incumbent**

The retrofit problem: bolting AI onto a mature product with existing habits and existing pricing.

**Angle:** When does AI belong as a feature versus a separate surface?

---

## Week 7 — Zerodha / Kite
**Oct 26–30 · Fintech (India)**

Zero-brokerage economics, no external funding, and a product that grew almost entirely without paid acquisition.

**Angle:** What did giving away the core product buy them, and what did it cost?

---

## Week 8 — Vercel
**Nov 2–6 · Dev tools**

Open source as the funnel, usage-based pricing as the meter. Both have sharp edges.

**Angle:** How do you commercialize a framework you gave away?

---

## Week 9 — Claude (Anthropic)
**Nov 9–13 · AI**

Three surfaces — consumer app, API, coding agent — with different users, different economics, and one model underneath.

**Angle:** How do you segment a product when the underlying capability is the same for everyone?

---

## Week 10 — UPI ecosystem
**Nov 16–20 · Fintech infrastructure**

Public rails at national scale with near-zero merchant discount rate. An unusual case where the infrastructure is deliberately not the profit centre.

**Angle:** If the rails are free, where does value get captured?

---

## Week 11 — Figma
**Nov 23–27 · B2B SaaS**

Multiplayer as a moat, and what happens to that moat when the generation of design shifts.

**Angle:** Does collaboration still defend a design tool in an AI-generated world?

---

## Week 12 — GitHub Copilot
**Nov 30 – Dec 4 · AI / dev tools**

Bookends Week 1. Same problem space, opposite starting position: distribution first instead of product first.

**Angle:** Distribution vs. product velocity — which one wins the coding-agent market?

---

## Week 13 — Synthesis
**Dec 7–11 · Essay**

No new product. Pull the pricing thread through all twelve teardowns into one essay, and polish the portfolio README into something worth linking from a résumé.

**Deliverable:** `notes/pricing-ai-products.md` + a rewritten front page.
