## Value-metric alignment

*From: (pending)*

The question to ask of any pricing page: **does the thing they charge for move in the same direction as the value the customer gets?**

- Aligned: a payments company charging per transaction
- Misaligned: a seat-priced tool whose value comes from automation that removes seats

Misalignment isn't fatal, but it predicts where the repricing pressure will come from.

## Deliberate friction vs. accidental friction

*From: (pending)*

Not every slow step is a bug. Before logging friction as a defect, ask what it might be protecting — risk, qualification, margin, support load. Getting this wrong is the most common error in a teardown, and the fastest way to sound junior.
