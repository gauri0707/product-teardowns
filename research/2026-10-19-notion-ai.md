## Three questions today's session should answer

1. In one quarter Notion closed a standalone surface (Mail), began exporting its Skills as `SKILL.md` into Claude Code and Cursor, and started letting customers pay for Notion's AI with their own ChatGPT subscription. Is that a company deciding its AI belongs inside other people's agents rather than its own — and if so, is "context layer" a better business than "24/7 AI team"?
2. AI is gated behind the $20 Business seat, but the part that scales with use is metered at $10/1,000 credits with no published per-action rate and no stated allowance. Which of the two is the product, and can a flat seat price hold next to a meter the buyer cannot forecast?
3. Notion's AI proof point is customer-level: >50% of ARR from "AI-enabled customers." What evidence would distinguish a retrofit that changed how work gets done from one that mainly changed which tier people sit on?
