## The week's biggest story is not about Razorpay

**India is ending zero-MDR on UPI.** From **15 October 2026**, person-to-merchant UPI transactions **above ₹2,000** carry a **0.4% MDR**, capped at **₹300** for transactions of ₹75,000 and above. Transactions at or below ₹2,000 stay at zero. Railways, telecom, insurance and fuel pay a **flat ₹5**; capital-markets transactions **0.02%** capped at ₹300. Merchants receiving up to **₹1 lakh/month** over UPI are exempt entirely (P2PM classification applies after three consecutive months above the line). P2P transfers remain free. ([Business Standard, 2026-09-24](https://www.business-standard.com/finance/news/mdr-on-upi-payments-what-customers-merchants-need-to-know-from-oct-15-126092401287_1.html); [Medianama, 2026-09-16](https://www.medianama.com/2026/09/223-upi-mdr-old-payments-debate/))

**NPCI's own framing, issued this week:** transactions up to ₹2,000 are *"more than 96% of UPI merchant transaction volume,"* and 18% GST applies to the fee, not the transaction value, with input tax credit available to GST-registered merchants. NPCI said reports that GST on MDR would make digital payments costlier for small businesses were *"incorrect."* ([Business Today, 2026-09-22](https://www.businesstoday.in/latest/economy/story/upi-mdr-gst-npci-says-small-merchants-and-96-of-transactions-remain-unaffected-from-october-15-557039-2026-09-22))

**Note the tension between two NPCI-adjacent numbers.** NPCI's 96%-of-*volume* figure and Goldman Sachs's estimate that **48% of total UPI P2M transaction *value*** would attract the full fee (August 2026 data) are not contradictory — they measure count vs. value — but they support opposite headlines. ([Business Standard, 2026-09-16](https://www.business-standard.com/industry/banking/upi-mdr-could-add-15-000-20-600-crore-to-fintechs-and-banks-annually-126091601015_1.html))

## Razorpay's own position, on its own pages

Razorpay published a UPI-MDR explainer **2026-09-22, updated 2026-09-24** — inside this week. It states that every gateway charges the **identical regulated NPCI rate** (0% to ₹2,000, 0.4% above, ₹300 cap) and that gateways compete on **platform fees and success rates, not the network rate**. Razorpay's own line is restated as **2% platform fee + 18% GST, zero setup, zero AMC**, with **90–95% success rates**, and it tells merchants the new MDR *"deduction on eligible transactions"* will appear in settlement files and requires reconciliation changes. ([razorpay.com/blog/lowest-upi-mdr-india-2026](https://razorpay.com/blog/lowest-upi-mdr-india-2026-what-merchants-actually-pay-per-payment-gateway), 2026-09-22/24)

## Merchant pushback, with a date attached

Madhya Pradesh Petroleum Dealers Association estimates **₹590/day per pump** (~100 transactions above ₹2,000) and says dealers will **refuse UPI above ₹2,000 from 16 October**; president Ajay Singh cites ~0.5% dealer margins. Retailers Association of India CEO Kumar Rajagopalan: the fee is a *"strong incentive to shift back to cash"* and *"UPI acceptance should be incentivized, not taxed."* An app, **one999**, reportedly splits payments into ₹1,999 chunks to stay under the threshold. ([Medianama, 2026-09-23](https://www.medianama.com/2026/09/223-merchants-push-back-against-upi-mdr/))
