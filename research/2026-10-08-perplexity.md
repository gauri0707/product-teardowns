## 1. Agent features moved behind credits — the dominant theme

Primary mechanics ([Perplexity Help Center, "How Credits Work"](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/13838041-how-credits-work-on-perplexity), read 2026-10-08, no last-updated date shown):

- **Pro ($20): no recurring monthly credits** — a one-time 4,000-credit bonus that expires in 30 days. **Max ($200): 10,000/mo plus a 35,000 one-time bonus.** Enterprise Pro 500/mo, Enterprise Max 15,000/mo.
- **100 credits = $1.** Auto-refill off by default; Max has a default $200/mo spend cap, adjustable to $5,000.
- Credits are consumed by **Computer tasks and Comet Assistant in Work mode**. Ask searches and Assistant in Search mode are free.
- Perplexity's own partial answer to the complaint: Pro "includes weekly Computer usage instead," **rolling out gradually, resets weekly, does not roll over**, separate from credits. **No announcement post, no published quantity, no date: not found.**
