## Pricing — Feb 2026, from Razorpay's own pages

Standard domestic rate is a flat **2% + 18% GST** on UPI, credit cards, debit cards, RuPay debit, netbanking and wallets alike. **No setup fee, no AMC**; charged only on successful transactions. Off the flat rate: Debit Card EMI **1%**; Credit Card on UPI **2.15%**; corporate cards, Pay Later, Cardless EMI and Amex/Diners **3%**. International cards **3%**, international wallets **3.5%**, international bank transfer **1%**. Subscriptions add **~0.99%**. ([razorpay.com/blog/razorpay-payment-gateway-pricing-explained](https://razorpay.com/blog/razorpay-payment-gateway-pricing-explained/), 2026-02-13)

On the UPI line specifically: bank-to-bank UPI carries a **government-mandated 0% MDR**, so Razorpay's 2% is recorded on that page as **"0% MDR + 2% platform fee."** UPI via RuPay credit card does carry MDR, *"approximately 1.1% to 2%,"* with a 2.15% platform fee above it. ([razorpay.com/learn/upi-transaction-charges](https://razorpay.com/learn/upi-transaction-charges/), 2026-02-10). That page records no 2025–26 proposal to reintroduce MDR on large merchants. Published enterprise/custom rate cards: **not found** — negotiated tiers are not disclosed.
