## Complaint themes, ranked by how often they recur

**1. Support latency, on every surface.** G2 reviewers report "customer support response time on lower-tier merchant accounts can sometimes take 24–48 hours via tickets" (23 Aug 2026) and "support chat could be faster, sometimes takes a day" (3 May 2026) ([G2](https://www.g2.com/products/razorpay/reviews)). Trustpilot is harsher: "never replies the mails, ticket raises or anything" (22 Jul 2026), "non-existent" support (7 Jul 2026) ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/razorpay.com)). Shopify reviewers describe support attempts that "created additional bugs" ([Shopify](https://apps.shopify.com/razorpay-checkout)). Capterra has a merchant citing 4+ months of promised "priority attention" with no resolution (Apr 2025) ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/179263/Razorpay/reviews/)).

**2. Settlements held for compliance/risk review, with no clock.** Trustpilot: "HOLDS SETTLEMENT STATING COMPLIANCE REVIEW" with funds withheld 120 days (30 May 2026). Capterra: "settlements can take a little longer than expected" (Jul 2026). Standard cycle is **T+2 working days**, and Razorpay's own docs say it "is subject to bank approval and can vary based on your business vertical, risk factor" ([Razorpay Docs, settlements FAQ](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/settlements/faqs/)). Razorpay's own blog publishes expected windows: KYC gaps "2 to 5 business days", chargeback reviews "1 to 3 weeks", fraud/legal holds "30 days or more", with freeze triggers including chargeback ratios crossing 1% and 10x volume spikes ([Razorpay blog, 9 Jun 2026, upd. 16 Jun 2026](https://razorpay.com/blog/what-to-do-when-your-payment-gateway-account-is-frozen/)). **Those windows are published in a blog post, not in the dashboard** — whether they appear in-product is *not found*; no crawlable source confirms either way.

**3. Onboarding / KYC friction.** Trustpilot: KYC fee charged before approval, then rejected without refund (17 Aug 2026). G2 cites a "strict account onboarding process" and "onboarding delays and payout issues during holidays and weekends". (KYC fee is ₹199 + tax — see Wednesday's file.)

**4. Dashboard sprawl.** G2, 23 Apr 2026: dashboard "has become very cluttered", "finding basic settings takes more time than it should". Capterra, Jan 2026: a merchant reports being unable to update basic business profile fields — "business picture, contact number, or WhatsApp details". Capterra, Aug 2026 (CFO): "Some information I needed to diagnose issues was not visible in the interface."

**5. Price.** G2 and Capterra both cite 2% as high for small businesses; G2 adds that "essential features require costly higher plans."

## What competitors already do about #1 and #2

- **Cashfree** — instant settlement "within 15 minutes, available for all business types"; promo 0% MDR up to ₹20L GMV/month to 31 Mar 2027.
- **Razorpay** — on-demand settlement "within 10 seconds" at **0.20–0.30%**, same-day at **0.15–0.20%**, ₹0 setup/AMC, available day 1 "for most businesses with a few exceptions and limits"; unlimited amounts only after "50 days" of quality transactions ([Razorpay Capital](https://razorpay.com/capital/instant-settlements/)). So speed is a paid add-on layered on top of a free-tier T+2.
- **PayU Priority Settlements** — 15 minutes, +0.25–0.50% on MDR.
- **CCAvenue Express Settlement** — same working day, 0.15–0.50% + tax.
- **Paytm PG** — T+1/T+2/same-day/on-demand; rates not publicly disclosed.

## Three questions today's session should answer

1. Trustpilot says 1.4, G2 says 4.2, and the Shopify listing is a 39/39 split between five- and one-star. Which population is the one whose problem is worth fixing — and does picking it change what you ship, or only who you'd tell about it?
2. Razorpay sells the fix for the second-loudest complaint: settlement speed is 0.15–0.30% away. If a held settlement is a risk decision and a slow settlement is a pricing decision, which of the two is the complaint thread actually about — and is a free "why, and until when" worth more to a merchant than a paid "now"?
3. The freeze windows Razorpay already publishes (2–5 days, 1–3 weeks, 30+ days) exist in a blog post. What would have to be true about their risk operations for those numbers to be unsafe to show inside the dashboard, next to the held payout?
