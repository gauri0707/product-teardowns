## The week's biggest news: rebuilt agents and a new credit system (Oct 19, 2026)

Notion rebuilt its agent harness and changed how AI is metered, both announced Oct 19.

- **Agent run length went from 5 minutes to 24+ hours.** Agents now get their own computer and browser, can write and run scripts, and have a "Review for me" mode where a separate model checks an action before execution. ([Notion blog, Oct 19 2026](https://www.notion.com/blog/notion-ai-more-for-less); [release notes, Oct 19 2026](https://www.notion.com/releases/2026-10-19))
- **Two credit pools replace the old allowance.** Business and Enterprise seats include **1,500 Standard credits/month** (Notion picks the model, via Auto) and **500 Select credits/month** (user picks the model, or runs Custom Agents). The pools are separate — not combinable, transferable, or exchangeable — and reset monthly with **no rollover**. ([Notion help: Manage your AI credits](https://www.notion.com/help/manage-your-ai-credits), retrieved Oct 23 2026)
- **Published per-task credit estimates.** Auto vs. Opus 5.5: simple task 5 vs. 50 credits; medium (transcribe + summarise a 1-hour meeting) 10 vs. 85; complex (create and configure databases) 25 vs. 150. ([Notion help](https://www.notion.com/help/manage-your-ai-credits))
- **Bring your own AI.** A ChatGPT Plus/Pro/Go plan can be connected to a *personal* Notion Agent (not Custom Agents); BYOK is in private alpha for Enterprise. Notion says "no markup," Business and Enterprise only. ([Notion blog, Oct 19 2026](https://www.notion.com/blog/notion-ai-more-for-less); [release notes, Oct 13 2026](https://www.notion.com/releases/2026-10-13))
- **Vendor-reported claims (unverified):** Auto matches "Opus 5.5 and Astra at ~90% lower average cost" across 20+ tasks; credits go ~10× further; Custom Agent runs ~30% cheaper; agents up to 50% faster; uptime >99.9%. Trade coverage notes the 90% figure is not independently verified. ([Notion blog](https://www.notion.com/blog/notion-ai-more-for-less); [Yahoo/Tech, Oct 22 2026](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/chatgpt/articles/notion-launches-gen-6-agent-000049770.html))
