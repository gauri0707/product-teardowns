## Linear's stated position on feature requests

Linear's own essay, *Building what customers need, not just what they ask for*
([2025-03-04](https://linear.app/now/building-what-customers-need)): feedback is "input, not
instructions." The worked example is **custom fields specifically** — they found ~40% of the people
asking wanted them to track customer needs, and shipped
[Customer Requests](https://linear.app/customer-requests) instead of generic fields. Customer
Requests ingests from Intercom, email, CRM, shared Slack channels and Linear Asks, and can
prioritise by revenue or tier. Plan gating not stated on the page.
