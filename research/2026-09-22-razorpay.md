## Journey A — merchant signup → activation (primary source: Razorpay Docs)

Documented order of steps, per [Create a Razorpay Account, Razorpay Docs](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/easy-submit-kyc/) (fetched 2026-09-22):

1. **Contact details** — 10-digit mobile → OTP → name.
2. **Platform details** — prior payments experience, where payments will be accepted (website/app, social media). Site links can be deferred.
3. **PAN and business details** — business type, PAN + name confirmation, registered business name, brand name, email.
4. **KYC** — business address and operational address, bank account + IFSC, GST (auto-filled if the PAN lookup succeeds), identity proof via DigiLocker (Aadhaar / Passport / Voter ID), business documents by entity type, business policy pages to review and publish, business category + subcategory.

These disagree in magnitude with Razorpay's own product-update claim: [Magic Checkout's New Single-Page Checkout](https://razorpay.com/blog/magic-checkouts-new-single-page-checkout/) (**2025-09-17**) says merchants can expect **"20–40 basis points"** of conversion lift — 20–40 extra sales per 10,000 checkout sessions. Agency case study [NetBramha — Razorpay Magic](https://www.netbramha.com/work/razorpay-magic) claims **"5× faster checkout"** and **"20% higher conversion"**, undated. Three sources, three very different numbers; the bps figure is the one Razorpay attaches to a specific shipped change.
