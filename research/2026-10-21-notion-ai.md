## The credit mechanism

From Notion's help centre ([Standard, Select & Notion credits](https://www.notion.com/help/standard-select-and-notion-credits-eligible-features), fetched 2026-10-21):

- **Standard — 1,500 per member/month.** Notion Agent on Auto, AI editing, translation, AI blocks, AI database formulas, AI Meeting Notes.
- **Select — 500 per member/month.** Notion Agent with a user-chosen model, and Custom Agents (the agent owner's balance pays by default).
- **Notion credits** — a shared, admin-managed workspace balance. Covers eligible usage after Standard/Select run out, and is the *only* type covering "high credit consumption models." Admins can fund a Custom Agent's monthly budget from it.
- Standard and Select are non-transferable, **reset monthly, do not roll over**; features pause when a bucket empties unless Notion credits are available.
- Only per-action figure published: **one hour of AI Meeting Notes ≈ 10 Standard credits.**
- AI Meeting Notes moves onto Standard credits **from Dec 1, 2026**. Workers (Beta), Database Autofill, and everyday Notion MCP/API read-write consume no credits.
