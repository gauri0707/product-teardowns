## The cost side: UPI MDR is changing in 3 weeks

This is the live variable under the model session.

- Today, MDR on UPI P2M is **zero** by government mandate, so Razorpay's 2% on UPI is a platform fee on a payment rail it pays ~nothing to use.
- New NPCI framework: **0.4% MDR on P2M UPI above ₹2,000**, capped at **₹300** for transactions ≥₹75,000; flat ₹5 for essential categories (fuel, telecom, railways, insurance, agri); 0% P2P and 0% P2M ≤₹2,000; small merchants (≤₹1 lakh/month via UPI QR) stay at zero. NPCI says **~96% of P2M transactions are unaffected**. ([CAclubindia summary of NPCI FAQ](https://www.caclubindia.com/news/npci-clarifies-gst-on-upi-mdr-small-merchants-and-96-percent-transactions-to-remain-unaffected-26806.asp); [Vajiram & Ravi, 21 Sep 2026](https://vajiramandravi.com/current-affairs/upi-mdr-2026/))
- **Sources disagree on the effective date:** CAclubindia says September 2026; Vajiram & Ravi and several trade outlets say **15 October 2026**. NPCI's own circular index could not be fetched (robots-disallowed), so the circular number and date are **not found**. Treat 15 Oct as reported-not-confirmed.
- Context on how contested this is: in June 2025 the Finance Ministry called reports of MDR on UPI "completely false, baseless and misleading." ([Tribune, 11 Jun 2025](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/finance-ministry-dismisses-speculation-of-mdr-of-upi-transactions-says-claims-false-baseless)) The position has since moved.

## Three questions today's session should answer

1. When 0.4% MDR lands on UPI above ₹2,000, does Razorpay's 2% platform fee become a pass-through, a margin squeeze, or a pricing-reset opportunity — and which merchant segment eats it?
2. Cashfree is 1.95% against Razorpay's 2%, with a promo an order of magnitude more generous (₹20L/month vs ₹5L cumulative). If price is that close and that public, what is the 2% actually buying — and would a merchant switching cost of near-zero show up anywhere in the FY25 numbers?
3. Gross profit grew 41% while revenue grew 65%. Which of the three plausible explanations — mix shift to lower-margin volume, promo discounting, or cost-to-serve rising — would you have to see to change your view of whether payments is the business or just the funnel?
